# Code of conduct

AI Worldline is built through evidence-based disagreement. Participants are expected to be respectful, specific, and generous when reviewing another person's work.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate misinformation, and repeated promotional submissions are not acceptable. Critique claims and sources rather than contributors.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Sensitive reports should be sent privately to the project maintainer through the contact method listed on their GitHub profile rather than posted in a public issue.
